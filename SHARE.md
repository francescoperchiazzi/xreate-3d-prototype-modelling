# Share XReate

Copy for LinkedIn and other social channels.

## LinkedIn

I made XReate for the moment an idea needs to leave the page and become a
volume. It is an experimental, browser-native 3D prototyping tool for
students, curious coders, and creative learners.

Draw a shape, give it depth, explore its surface, and export it as a small
scene prop, scenographic study, game-space idea, or immersive XR experiment.
The workflow stays deliberately visible: volume, scale, UV mapping, texture,
and export are all there to be understood rather than hidden behind a large
professional suite.

Cinema and theatre have always taught us that imagination becomes believable
when it has space, material, light, and proportion. XReate is a small place to
practice that craft through making, observing, revising, and sharing.

Everything runs in the browser. No account is required.

## An honest note

XReate is experimental, so a few bugs or rough edges may remain. Nobody is
perfect: save often, test your export, and share feedback kindly.
