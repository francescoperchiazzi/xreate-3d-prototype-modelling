# For Federico

![Federico Torre](assets/federico-torre.jpg)

XReate is an experimental browser-native 3D prototyping tool for coders. It
is not a general-purpose 3D suite: it is a place to prototype scene props,
scenography, gameplay spaces, and spatial studies for videogames and immersive
XR experiences through direct manipulation of simple volumes.

Space is not decoration around an interface. Space itself is the interface.

The project values fast iteration, visible UV choices, and enough constraints
to make volume, surface, texture, and export legible to a person new to 3D.
It deliberately avoids the ambition of an “everything editor.”

XReate is dedicated to Federico Torre — as a quiet acknowledgement of an
influence: cinema, theatre, scenography, and the discipline of bodies in space.

Federico Torre is an Italian actor, born in Naples on 29 May 1958. His work has
spanned theatre, radio, cinema, and television, after acting training in New
York at the Theater Lab and a return to Italy in the late 1990s. His filmography
includes work with directors such as Paolo Sorrentino, Tom Tykwer, Antonietta
De Lillo, and Marco Bellocchio.
For a fuller biographical and filmographic record, see the
[Italian Wikipedia article](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federico_Torre_(attore))
and its linked [Wikimedia Commons media](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Federico_Torre).

The portrait above is included with attribution in
[assets/ATTRIBUTION.md](assets/ATTRIBUTION.md).

This remains an experimental teaching tool. Its value is not perfection; it is
the possibility of making, observing, and learning with care.

> “Cinema captured me when I was young and never let me go.
>
> I learned this craft between the pavement and New York: by living, observing,
> and studying at the Theater Lab.
>
> Because cinema, before anything else, is life.
>
> And this life continues in those I met, in those who cared for me.”

The quotation remains in English by design. The web app uses AI-assisted
translation drafts with an explicit fallback and disclosure; see
[docs/LOCALIZATION.md](docs/LOCALIZATION.md).
